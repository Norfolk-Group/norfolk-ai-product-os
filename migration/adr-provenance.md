# Imported ADR provenance

Kit ADRs 0001–0017 were imported from pinned commit `f2680624596048a88f78e2a77b2a7e3d1d4aeed5`. [`imported-adrs.json`](imported-adrs.json) stores the source path, original date, exact body SHA-256, imported path, and historical disposition.

Bodies are byte-for-byte source copies. Their original relative links may no longer resolve in Product OS and are intentionally not rewritten; rewriting would falsify the preserved body. Some bodies contain client-context examples and are restricted history, not reusable canonical guidance. A current decision must be authored in the top-level decision sequence and may cite, accept, reject, or supersede the imported record.

Imported numbering is namespaced under `decisions/imported/norfolk-kit/`; it never competes with Product OS decision numbers.
