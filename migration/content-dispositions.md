# Migration content dispositions

| Source family | Disposition | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Kit universal governance and rationale | Promote | Re-authored as Product OS governance, decisions, product method, and design contracts with source register links |
| Kit executable stack, adapters, UI, checks | Implement | Remains executable in Kit; Product OS owns WHAT and WHY |
| Kit ADRs 0001–0017 | Historical import | Exact bodies live under `decisions/imported/norfolk-kit/`; they do not enter the current decision sequence |
| Kit animation sources and Replit preservation | Implement + provenance | Remain in Kit; lineage and approval rules live in Product OS |
| Kit client-brand assets and large masters | Leave behind | Release blocker pending rights classification and separate versioned storage; never copied into Product OS or future applications |
| Manual duplicate governance, agent files, animation specs/source | Supersede | Product OS and Kit replacements exist; duplicates are not current authority |
| Manual unique setup/launcher tools | Evaluate | Preserve until parity-tested in Kit; Manual cannot retire while unique value remains |
| Starter Clerk/Prisma/Next.js implementation | Supersede | Archived legacy reference; current Kit uses WorkOS, Drizzle, and its approved reference architecture |
| Starter agent-native commands and skills | Evaluate | Candidate input for U7; no automatic promotion |
| H+ design, agent, progress, and animation architecture | Promote sanitized doctrine | Reuse taxonomy, truthful attribution, server-configured registries, and quality gates; keep product names, personas, visual identities, palette, and business rules product-local |

Product blanks become templates. Product- or client-specific identity, palettes, business rules, evidence, and credentials remain outside canonical content.
