# Product discovery

Discovery produces an approved product profile and goals; it does not produce screens or implementation scope.

## Required product profile

Record:

- product name and purpose;
- users, buyers or payers, and accountable product owner;
- jobs and decisions the product supports;
- value created for the user and organization;
- measurable success outcomes and current baseline when known;
- domain vocabulary and constraints;
- trust, emotional, brand, and accessibility posture;
- data sensitivity, legal, security, and integration constraints;
- whether an existing UI is available for observation.

Unknowns remain explicit. Do not convert an assumption into a fact because the workflow wants a complete field.

## Evidence sources

Use owner interviews, user interviews, existing product and business documents, analytics, support evidence, approved research, and direct system observation. Label the source, date, rights, sensitivity, confidence, and any conflict. Client-derived material follows the client-boundary gate before it enters Product OS evidence.

## Exit gate

Discovery is complete when the accountable product owner accepts the purpose, audience, value, decisions, success measures, trust posture, constraints, and existing-versus-greenfield state. Conflicting goals are resolved or recorded as an explicit decision before observation begins.
