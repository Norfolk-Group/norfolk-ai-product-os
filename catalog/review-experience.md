# Catalog review experience

The landing view prioritizes pending diffs, blocked evidence, and stale catalogs before stable specimens. Reviewers can search, filter, deep-link, compare states/themes/breakpoints side by side, freeze or replay motion, enable reduced motion, inspect provenance and accessibility metadata, and enter from a CI diff.

Every decision offers **approve**, **reject**, or **defer**, requires a rationale, records the accountable reviewer, and provides an explicit exit path. Approval of an artifact does not silently approve a changed design contract or visual baseline; each consequential decision remains separately attributable.
