# Output system

Generated business materials are product surfaces with their own design, data, authorization, confidentiality, lifecycle, and verification contracts. They are not screenshots of the application and do not inherit safe behavior by accident.

Read [shared principles](shared-principles.md) and [job lifecycle](job-lifecycle.md). Presentation-producing systems also apply the Norfolk-owned [Presentation IR](presentation-ir.md) before the format contract: [PDF](pdf.md), [XLSX](xlsx.md), [PPTX](pptx.md), [DOCX](docx.md), [email](email.md), [charts](charts.md), [print](print.md), or [investor materials](investor-materials.md).
