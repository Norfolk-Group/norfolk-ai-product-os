# DOCX

DOCX is paged but editable. Use semantic heading, list, table, caption, header/footer, footnote, and cross-reference styles. Keep-with-next and controlled page breaks prevent orphan headings. Long paragraphs and tables reflow; no text boxes are used for primary reading order. Tracked-change policy, template version, language, accessibility, fields, sources, and final PDF parity are declared.
