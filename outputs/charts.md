# Charts

Every chart names measure, units, currency/scale, population, source, as-of/date basis, aggregation, precision, and missing-data behavior. Encodings do not rely on color alone. Axes and baselines do not mislead; dual axes require explicit approval. Empty, partial, stale, negative, zero, outlier, and dense-label states are fixtures. Each chart has an accessible text alternative describing the finding and the underlying data table when disclosure permits.
