# PPTX

Slides are fixed canvases with declared aspect ratio, safe area, typography floor, theme, and speaker-note policy. One message dominates each slide. Long titles recompose or split; they never shrink without limit. Tables move to appendix/detail slides before becoming unreadable. Charts retain units, source, date basis, and editable data when allowed. Font substitution, overflow, off-slide objects, reading order, contrast, notes, and export-to-PDF are verified.
