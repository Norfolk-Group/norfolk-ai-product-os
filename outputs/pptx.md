# PPTX

PPTX generation starts from the Norfolk-owned [presentation IR](presentation-ir.md): durable semantics are authored and validated before vendor-neutral render geometry is derived. Slides are fixed canvases with declared aspect ratio, safe area, typography floor, theme, and speaker-note policy. One message dominates each slide. Long titles recompose or split; they never shrink without limit. Tables move to appendix/detail slides before becoming unreadable. Charts retain units, source, date basis, and editable data when allowed. Font substitution, overflow, off-slide objects, native reading order, contrast, notes, renderer identity, deterministic output hash, and export-to-PDF are verified.
