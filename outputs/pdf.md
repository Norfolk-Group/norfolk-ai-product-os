# PDF

PDF is a fixed, paged canvas. Body text is at least 8pt and contracts should choose 9–11pt; margins are at least 18pt. Tables repeat headers, split only at governed row boundaries, and move to landscape or appendices before type shrinks below the floor. Page numbers, document title, language, reading order, bookmarks, tagged structure, alt text, source notes, confidentiality, and generated-at basis are explicit. Missing fonts or glyphs fail rendering; silent substitution is prohibited.
