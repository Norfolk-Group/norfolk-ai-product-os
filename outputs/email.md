# Email

Email is a fluid, hostile-client canvas. The subject, preheader, sender identity, reply path, audience authorization, confidentiality, plain-text alternative, unsubscribe/legal obligations, link destination, and expiry are explicit. Core meaning survives blocked images and narrow screens. Tables are presentation-only and responsive; attachments follow their own format contracts. Delivery is a human-only consequential action unless a separately approved automation contract says otherwise.
