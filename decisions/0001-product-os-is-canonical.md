# 0001 — Product OS is canonical

## Context

Norfolk AI product knowledge accumulated across conversations, Kit documents and ADRs, copied Manual material, product repositories, design artifacts, and historical starters. Norfolk Kit previously described itself as canonical, but an executable starter cannot safely be both portfolio doctrine and one implementation of that doctrine.

## Decision

`Norfolk-Group/norfolk-ai-product-os` is Norfolk AI's canonical authority for product doctrine, design, governance, architecture, security, data, agent-native behavior, outputs, decision rationale, and adoption contracts.

Norfolk AI owns the Product OS and reusable intellectual property. Client and product repositories—including KIT Capital accounts and H-Analytics—may supply reviewed evidence but are not automatic canon.

## Consequences

- Product OS owns WHAT and WHY and publishes versioned releases.
- Consumers adopt a pinned release; newer unadopted releases do not govern them.
- Existing Kit doctrine is valuable migration evidence and superseded history, not a simultaneous editable canonical source after cutover.
- Durable knowledge must be indexed, tiered, owned, freshness-aware, and explicitly superseded.
- Migration may establish retirement readiness but does not authorize deletion.

## Supersedes

This decision supersedes the prior claim that Norfolk Kit itself is the portfolio's canonical source. The original Kit records will be preserved with provenance during the approved migration unit rather than rewritten here.
