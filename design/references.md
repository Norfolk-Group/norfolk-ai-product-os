# Design references

A reference record moves through `named` → `observed` → `extracted` → `accepted`, or to `rejected`/`superseded`. Naming a product or aesthetic creates no rule. Observation records the actual source. Extraction states the transferable pattern. Acceptance names scope, evidence, reviewer, and conflicts.

## Current accepted extractions

| Reference | State | Accepted extraction |
|---|---|---|
| Manus sidebar evidence | accepted | Content-type icons may vary while library, weight, size, and color stay locked; homogeneous name lists do not need repeated icons. |
| Perplexity desktop evidence | accepted | A conversational surface has one hero—the bounded composer—with quiet inline controls and restrained suggestions. |
| Linear workspace evidence | accepted | Dense operational lists use a stable row grid, form-plus-text state, quiet labels, and minimal group chrome. |
| Richard Thaler and David Brooks bodies of work | accepted | Plain, concrete language that respects the reader, explains through cases, stays warm without cheerleading, and admits uncertainty. |

These are narrow extractions, not instructions to copy a product's brand or layout wholesale.

## Named or incomplete references

Cloudflare's assistant, Codex workflow, ElevenLabs, Qurrent AI, and any other unexamined reference remain `named` until evidence is captured and reviewed. Blank is better than plausible invention.

## Motion provenance

Figma Make, Replit exports, H-Analytics production behavior, and Kit copies are lineage sources rather than ordinary inspiration. Preserve all variants; U9 will record hashes, availability, visual intent, production behavior, and the reason a promoted implementation wins.
