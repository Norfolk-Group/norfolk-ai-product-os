# Design provenance

Every catalog specimen and motion record names its source path, normalized SHA-256, source role, repository or local archive, Product OS and Kit versions, approval state, freshness, and supersession relationship. Client-derived sources also require reuse rights, sanitization, identifier and secret scans, and disclosure review.

Original editable masters, implementation exports, deployed product variants, and portable reference variants are immutable evidence. Reconciliation creates governed derivatives; it does not rewrite, relocate, archive, or delete the original. Object-storage preservation is complete only after a version identifier is recorded and retrieval reproduces the expected checksum.
