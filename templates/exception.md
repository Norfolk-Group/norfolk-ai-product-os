# EX-0000 — Exception title

Record state, owner, product, affected standard, exact scope, rationale/evidence, risk, compensating controls, approver, approval date, review/expiry date, and migration consequence. Unknown, expired, or unapproved exceptions fail compliance and remain visible during re-adoption.
