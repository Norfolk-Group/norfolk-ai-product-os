# PP-0000 — Promotion title

Record lifecycle state, source commit/artifact and provenance, Norfolk ownership or reuse license, sensitivity, synthetic replacement, metadata/identifier/secret scans, human disclosure review, evidence, confidence, impact, affected contracts, compatibility, reviewer, and decision. A product opens a proposal; it cannot modify canon directly. Client-restricted or unknown-rights material cannot be accepted.
