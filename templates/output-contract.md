# Output contract

- ID, format, purpose, audience, owner, confidentiality, and authorization
- Canvas, page/slide/sheet dimensions, margins, typography floor, and font files
- Pagination and overflow behavior for long title, paragraph, cell, table, and labels
- Authoritative input/calculation snapshot, locale, currency, timezone, rounding, disclosures, generated-at time, and version
- Chart units, sources, date basis, missing-data behavior, and text alternative
- Accessibility, language, reading order, tagging, contrast, and plain-text alternative
- Generated-asset policy, R2 version/checksum, rights, and provenance
- Signed delivery, expiry, revocation, retry, cancellation, and idempotency
- Golden fixtures: normal, overflow, empty, unavailable, partial/stale, font substitution, wide/dense, and error

Complete the machine-readable [`output-contract.schema.json`](../schemas/output-contract.schema.json) and [`output-job.schema.json`](../schemas/output-job.schema.json).
