# Decision title

## Status and scope

Proposed/accepted/superseded; owner; decision date; last verification; affected contracts; compatibility and semantic-version consequence.

## Context and evidence

State the need, constraints, official/current evidence, assumptions, and expiry of time-sensitive facts.

## Decision

State the chosen rule, boundaries, and accountable owner.

## Alternatives and rejection

Record meaningful alternatives, including the status quo, and why each was rejected.

## Consequences and reversal

Record benefits, costs, risks, migration, rollback/recovery, reversal triggers, and the evidence required to reconsider.

## Supersession

Link the decision superseded and preserve its body. A later record supersedes this one; this file is not rewritten into a different history.
