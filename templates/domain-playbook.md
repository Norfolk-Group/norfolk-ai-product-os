# Domain playbook template

Keep status `planned` until evidence exists. Empty is more trustworthy than invented guidance.

## Record

- ID and domain:
- Status: `planned`, `evidence-review`, `accepted`, `deprecated`, or `superseded`
- Owner and last verified:
- Domain vocabulary and decisions:
- Users, roles, trust, risk, data, and output posture:

## Claims

For each claim record:

- stable ID;
- scope: `universal`, `domain`, or `product-pattern`;
- statement;
- evidence references and rights/sensitivity state;
- evidence strength: `none`, `single-example`, `multi-product`, `research`, or `authoritative`;
- confidence;
- known counterexamples and permitted deviations.

- Decision: `accepted`, `rejected`, or `deferred`
